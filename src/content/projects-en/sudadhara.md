problem: |
  An independent therapist depended on word of mouth and had nowhere to
  explain the method before the first contact, which pushed the whole
  explanation into the initial conversation.
decisions: |
  Single page with the method explained before the call to contact,
  instead of a multi-page site nobody would walk through. Direct contact,
  with no form or integrated calendar, because the volume did not justify
  the maintenance.
outcome: |
  Stayed online in 2025 under its own domain, which went offline. The
  repository is closed, so there is no link to offer.
